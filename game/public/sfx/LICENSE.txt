Original filenames, for anyone who wants to swap in a sibling from the pack:

  catch.wav    <- powerUp7.ogg    (rising arpeggio, 0.29s)
  deliver.wav  <- pepSound1.ogg   (falling blip, 0.33s)
  boost.wav    <- phaseJump2.ogg  (rising whoosh, 0.32s)

Decoded to mono 16-bit 44.1kHz WAV because Safari decodes no Ogg Vorbis, then
trimmed to the audible part with 4ms fades at each end.

All three are from the same pack, recorded on one KORG microKORG, which is
why they sound like one set rather than three found sounds.
